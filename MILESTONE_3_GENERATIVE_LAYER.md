# Script Lab — Milestone 3: Generative Creative Brain

Status: Protected baseline

Milestone 3 marks the first live generative layer that produced materially more diverse creative routes from the same brief.

## What changed

The Creative Brain moved from deterministic route patterns toward a generative architecture:

Brief -> audience/product/context -> human truth -> creative territories -> choose distinct mechanisms -> filmable scene -> dialogue -> product integration -> benefit/payoff -> CTA -> self-critique.

The model must think through the brief before writing the five scripts and privately reject weak or repetitive routes.

## Milestone 3 learnings

1. Generate ideas before scripts. The creative idea comes before scene wording.
2. Five routes must be genuinely different underlying ideas, not one proposition placed in five settings.
3. Creative diversity changes the human observation, behaviour, tension, device, reveal, relationship dynamic or payoff.
4. Audience context and product truth are separate layers.
5. Audience information can shape behaviour and world-building, but eligibility, offer mechanics, benefits and programme rules come from the brief.
6. When the brief explicitly states a product mechanism, use it confidently and do not dilute or incorrectly challenge it.
7. Prefer a simple, human situation over an over-engineered creative device.
8. The creative device must emerge from the human truth and product truth.
9. Product integration must feel causally earned and may happen through dialogue, behaviour, visual demonstration, super, VO or payoff.
10. Dialogue must be caused by the situation and by what another character has said or done.
11. Compare the five routes before returning them. Replace routes that share substantially the same premise, discovery mechanism, reveal or payoff.
12. Test specificity: if the same story could work for many unrelated products after only changing the product name, rewrite it.
13. Judge the finished output as a film: filmable action, believable behaviour, spoken dialogue, visual storytelling, product clarity, human payoff and timing.
14. Creative zones are lenses, not plot instructions.
15. Reference films teach transferable principles, not templates, phrase banks or fixed beat structures.
16. Do not respond to weak output by continuously accumulating rules. Diagnose whether the weakness is in brief extraction, ideation, territory selection, story construction, dialogue causality, product integration or self-critique, then improve that layer.
17. Product entry and product explanation are different. The product can become relevant early without forcing an explanatory line into the opening.
18. Features should use the storytelling mechanism most natural to the particular idea; not every feature needs dialogue.
19. Specificity means circumstances, behaviour, objects, stakes and relationships — not adjectives.
20. Write the scene, not the strategy behind the scene.
21. Characters need a reason to be present and a relationship or energy that creates believable interaction.
22. Humour, emotion, surprise, contrast, misdirection, escalation, role reversal, visual metaphor and other devices are optional creative lenses, never mandatory structures.
23. Speaker identity must remain clear.
24. Finished scripts must not contain internal creative labels or strategy commentary.
25. Every route must contain exactly six real film rows for the selected duration format; the first row must be an actual shot, not column headings.

## Live benchmark observed

The credit-card test brief generated five materially different mechanisms rather than five versions of the same discovery story. This is the benchmark for future changes.

The output is not considered finished or perfect. The next goal is to improve creative quality without losing the diversity and generative behaviour achieved here.

## Protection rule

Milestone 1 remains the rollback baseline.

Milestone 3 is the current generative benchmark.

Future patches must be additive and tested against this benchmark. Do not replace this architecture with a larger static rule list.
